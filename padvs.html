Designed for Controlled Participation

PADVS is built around the principle that high-volume participation programmes require more than a consumer interface. They require a controlled operating environment encompassing transactions, rules, administration, security, reconciliation, auditability and reporting.

Platform + Programme

IGSL owns and develops the PADVS technology and underlying architecture. Market-specific programmes can then be originated and operated through appropriately structured local entities and partnerships.
