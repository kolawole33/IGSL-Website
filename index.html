FLAGSHIP PROGRAMMES

PUBLIC DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE
SAIP

State Addressing & Identity Programme

A comprehensive framework integrating physical addressing, geospatial information, digital systems and institutional processes to create the location-intelligence infrastructure required by modern government.

Currently being implemented for a state in Southern Nigeria, SAIP demonstrates how addressing can evolve from street identification into persistent digital public infrastructure.
