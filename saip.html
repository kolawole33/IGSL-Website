Building the Infrastructure of Location.

SAIP - State Addressing & Identity Programme

Reliable addressing is fundamental infrastructure. It enables governments to identify locations and properties, emergency services to respond effectively, businesses to reach customers and citizens to connect more efficiently with public and private services.

IGSL is providing programme management and technical advisory leadership for the implementation of a comprehensive State Addressing & Identity Programme for a state in Southern Nigeria.

More Than Addresses

SAIP integrates:

01 Physical Addressing
02 Geospatial Data
03 Property Identification
04 Digital Address Infrastructure
05 Field Operations
06 Institutional Systems
07 Data Management

The programme establishes a structured location-information framework rather than simply installing street signs and house numbers.

From Addressing to Location Intelligence

SAIP creates infrastructure capable of supporting:

01 Urban Planning
02 Emergency Response
03 Revenue Administration
04 Property & Land Administration
05 Utilities & Infrastructure
06 Logistics & Commerce
07 Financial / Location Verification
08 Government Service Delivery
Digital Public Infrastructure

Digital Public Infrastructure

At its digital core, SAIP provides the institutional framework through which addressing and spatial information can be continuously maintained, accessed and used. This transforms addressing from a one-time exercise into persistent location-enabled digital infrastructure.

Adaptable Across States

One framework. Different local realities.

Every state has different geography, institutions, existing data and policy priorities. SAIP is therefore structured as an adaptable programme framework rather than a rigid one-size-fits-all product.

IGSL works with state institutions to configure the programme around local requirements while maintaining the fundamental architecture required for a sustainable addressing and location-intelligence system.
